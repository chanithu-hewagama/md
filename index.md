# Discrete Transistor T Latch — Sri Lanka Component List & Cost Estimate

To build a T Latch using discrete components (2× AND gates and 2× NOR gates), here is the breakdown tailored to availability and pricing from electronic component vendors in Sri Lanka (e.g., Scion Electronics, Tronic.lk, Microchip.lk).

---

## 1. Core Electronics Components

* **NPN BJ Transistors:** **8 pcs**
  * *Recommended Model:* `2N3904` or `BC547` (TO-92 package)
  * *Purpose:* 4 transistors for 2 AND gates (2 each) + 4 transistors for 2 NOR gates (2 each)

* **Base Resistors ($R_1$):** **8 pcs**
  * *Recommended Value:* `10 kΩ` (1/4 Watt, 5% Carbon Film)
  * *Purpose:* Base current limiting

* **Load/Pull Resistors ($R_2$):** **4 pcs**
  * *Recommended Value:* `1 kΩ` to `10 kΩ` (1/4 Watt)
  * *Purpose:* 2 pull-down resistors for AND outputs + 2 pull-up resistors for NOR outputs

---

## 2. Prototyping Gear & Power

* **Solderless Breadboard:** 1 pc (400 or 830 tie-point / MB-102)
* **Jumper Wires:** 1 set (Male-to-Male jumper wires, 10 cm or 20 cm)
* **Power Supply:** 5V DC source (e.g., 5V USB cable, 3× AA battery holder, or standard bench supply)
